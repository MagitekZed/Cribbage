Cribbage style guide

Every surface in the game, off the felt and out of a running hand. Nothing on this page is an image file.

Type scale

Two system stacks, no downloads. The serif carries card indices and every score numeral; the sans handles all UI chrome. The six sizes are declared at :root in layout.css and are a candidate for promotion into theme.css.

Colour tokens

Read live out of theme.css at page load, so this table cannot drift from the stylesheet. Each chip sits half over cream and half over near-black, which is how the tokens carrying alpha give themselves away.

Dimensions

Resolved against the current viewport — resize the window and reload to see the clamps move. Card geometry all derives from --card-w; board geometry all derives from --hole-size.

Motion

One multiplier, --anim-scale, drives every duration in the game, and prefers-reduced-motion drops it to 0.001. If the durations below read as near-zero, that is your OS setting, not a bug.

The deck — all 52

Rows are the four suits in card-id order (spades, hearts, diamonds, clubs), columns are ace through king. Every pip is an inline SVG <use> of one of four symbols. The courts are an art deco panel drawn in one inline SVG on the card’s own 5:7 viewBox — no figures, no image files — and the ace of spades gets the oversized treatment a printed deck traditionally gives it.

The courts, close up

One vocabulary, three silhouettes: the King is a mitred square around a solid stepped crown with the suit knocked out of it, the Queen a circle under a rising fan, the Jack a deep-cut lozenge under two chevrons. Every motif is built once and cloned through a half turn, so a court reads the same either way up — only the rank letter stays upright. Stroke widths are in SVG user units, so the fine rules thin out with the card instead of clogging: the row underneath is the same four cards at the sizes the game actually deals.

The deck back

Both players draw from one deck, so the back is a single global choice and never a per-player tell: there is no back argument anywhere in the card API. The colour comes from --card-back / --card-back-dark, which theme.css resolves from [data-deck-back] — an ordinary attribute selector, so it works on the document root in the game and on a wrapper here. The four below are locked to their own colour; the buttons set the attribute on <html>, which is exactly what a settings screen will do, and every other face-down card on this page follows it.

Deck back

Red is the :root default, so theme.css declares no rule for it — this page re-asserts one locally, or the red card would follow the picker like everything else.

Card states

Every state carries a cue that is not colour: selected rises, highlighted grows a solid ring, unplayable gets a hatch, face-down announces itself as face-down rather than as its identity.

Tab through the interactive cards to see the focus ring.

The board

The holes are drilled ellipses and the pegs are turned metal seen from the same raised camera — one squash ratio foreshortens every hole and every ellipse in the peg together, which is what stops the board reading as a plan view with side-on pegs standing on it. Each pair below is chosen for its peg geometry: a fresh board stacks both pegs in the start holes, the second is a one-point peg where the two are in adjacent holes, the third is a big count that throws them most of a street apart, and the fourth straddles the skunk line at 91. The last is a 61-point board, whose skunk line moves to 31 with it.

The rear peg is where the score stood before the last award; the board does the leapfrog itself.
