Cribbage style guide

Every surface in the game, off the felt and out of a running hand. Nothing on this page is an image file.

Type scale

Two system stacks, no downloads. The serif carries card indices and every score numeral; the sans handles all UI chrome. The six sizes are declared at :root in layout.css and are a candidate for promotion into theme.css.

Colour tokens

Read live out of theme.css at page load, so this table cannot drift from the stylesheet. Each chip sits half over cream and half over near-black, which is how the tokens carrying alpha give themselves away.

Dimensions

Resolved against the current viewport — resize the window and reload to see the clamps move. Card geometry all derives from --card-w; board geometry all derives from --hole-size.

Motion

One multiplier, --anim-scale, drives every duration in the game, and prefers-reduced-motion drops it to 0.001. If the durations below read as near-zero, that is your OS setting, not a bug.

The deck — all 52

Rows are the four suits in card-id order (spades, hearts, diamonds, clubs), columns are ace through king. Every pip is an inline SVG <use> of one of four symbols; the courts are a typographic panel, not an illustration.

Backs and states

Every state carries a cue that is not colour: selected rises, highlighted grows a solid ring, unplayable gets a hatch, face-down announces itself as face-down rather than as its identity.

Tab through the interactive cards to see the focus ring.

The board

Four boards at different score pairs. The third straddles the skunk line at 91 — the pone is short of it, the dealer is past it. The fourth is a 61-point board, where the skunk line moves to 31 with it.

Screen furniture

The pieces layout.css owns, shown against felt. The two notes panels are shown filled here; on the game screen they ship empty, because Phase 4 is what puts rows in them.
